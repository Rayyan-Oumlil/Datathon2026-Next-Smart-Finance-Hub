# Volatility

Volatility measures how much a price moves up and down. In this app it is the standard deviation of daily returns over one year, scaled to a yearly figure (multiplied by the square root of 252 trading days).

- A volatility of 20% means that, over the past year, the price moved in a way consistent with yearly swings of about 20% around its average path.
- Broad-index funds have tended to show lower volatility than single growth companies, and crypto-assets higher.
- Volatility is computed from past prices. It describes the past year, not the future.

Each risk profile in this app has a volatility cap: Low 20%, Medium 35%, High 80%. An asset above the cap for your profile is not listed on your smart watchlist.
