# Diversification

Diversification means spreading money across assets that do not all move together, for example several sectors or asset classes, so that one fall has a smaller effect on the whole. It reduces the effect of a single asset; it does not remove the possibility of loss.

In this app the watchlist lists at most two picks per sector, and one factor of the score is higher for an asset in a sector or asset class the simulated portfolio does not hold yet.
