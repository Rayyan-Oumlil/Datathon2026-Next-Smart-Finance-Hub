# Drawdown (largest fall)

A drawdown is the fall in value from a peak to the lowest point that followed, before a new peak is reached. It is written as a percentage of the peak.

- If a portfolio rises to 100 and then falls to 80, its drawdown is 20%.
- In this app the "largest fall" (the maximum drawdown) is the deepest such fall over the period shown, with the date of the peak and the date of the low point.
- A drawdown describes a past period only. It does not say what will happen next.

The X-ray, the replay of a strategy and the drawdown guard rule all use this measure.
