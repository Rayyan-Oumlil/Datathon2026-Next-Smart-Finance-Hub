# Bond yield

A bond is a loan to a government or a company. Its yield is the yearly return an investor gets at today's price if they hold it to the end. When a bond's price goes up, its yield goes down, and the reverse.

The Government of Canada benchmark yields shown in this app (2, 5 and 10 years) come from the Bank of Canada. They are yields, not prices of a product you can hold in the simulated portfolio.
