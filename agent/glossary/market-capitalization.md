# Market capitalization (market cap)

Market cap is the share price multiplied by the number of shares: the total market value of a company. Common size bands are large cap (US$10 billion or more), mid cap (US$2 billion to US$10 billion) and small cap (US$300 million to US$2 billion).

In this app each risk profile has a market-cap floor: Low US$10 billion, Medium US$2 billion, High US$300 million. Canadian market caps are converted to US dollars with the Bank of Canada rate of the day before the comparison.
