# Smart watchlist and score

The smart watchlist is a ranked list of assets built by fixed rules, not by the AI.

1. Candidates come from the sectors, niches, themes and brands in your profile.
2. Filters remove assets that do not match your profile: your avoid list, the volatility cap and market-cap floor of your risk profile, a minimum traded value per day, and the ESG filter when you picked the ESG theme.
3. Each remaining asset gets a score from 0 to 100 built from four parts: profile fit (40%), risk fit (25%), fundamentals (20%) and diversification (15%).
4. The list keeps the top assets, with at most two per sector, and changes slowly: it is recomputed once a day.

The score describes how closely an asset matches the profile you gave. It is not a forecast of its price and not advice. Every value used is stored with its source and time; "Why?" on a card shows them.
