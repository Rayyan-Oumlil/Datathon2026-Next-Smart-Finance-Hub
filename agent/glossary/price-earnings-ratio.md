# P/E ratio (price-to-earnings)

The P/E ratio is the share price divided by the company's earnings per share over the last twelve months.

- A P/E of 40 means the price is 40 times one year of earnings per share.
- A P/E is usually compared with other companies in the same sector, because typical levels differ between sectors.
- A higher P/E than the sector means investors are paying more for each dollar of current earnings, often because they expect earnings to grow. A lower P/E means they are paying less.
- A P/E cannot be computed when earnings are negative.

A P/E describes the current price against past earnings. It does not say what the price will do next.

In this app the P/E comes from Finnhub (field peTTM) and is compared with the median P/E of the candidates in the same sector.
